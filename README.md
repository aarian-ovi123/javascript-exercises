# JavaScript Exercises
JavaScript exercises from my programming classes.

- **Height in Meters** – Converts heights from feet and inches into meters.
- **Area of Triangle** – Calculates a triangle's area from its base and height.
- **Triple** – A function that multiplies a number by three.
- **Square** – A function that multiplies a number by itself.
- **Ride Simulation** – Simulates amusement park rides for however many tickets you buy, then awards a prize based on how many rides you enjoyed.
- **Disney World** – Decides whether you can go to Disney World today based on your ticket, school night and money.
- **Meal Planner** – Recommends a meal for breakfast, lunch or dinner.
- **School's Out** – Figures out whether there's school today based on weekdays and holidays.
- **Can You Graduate?** – Checks whether you have enough credits and met the requirements to graduate.
- **Hello, World** – Prints a greeting with my name and favorite hobby.
- **Running Speed** – Calculates your running speed in miles per hour from distance and time.
-  **Return Values** – `square` and `quadruple` functions that return their results instead of printing them.
- **Inventory** – Tracks a store's inventory as the user buys items, rejecting purchases that are too big or invalid.
- **Min** – A function that returns the smaller of two numbers.
- **Even or Odd?** – Tells whether each number you enter is even or odd, until you enter 0.
- **Logical Operators** – Demonstrates NOT, AND and OR with a light switch, presidential requirements and social media.
- **Obi-Wan Says** – Asks your name and how many droids and Wookiees you want to meet.
- **Factorial** – Calculates N! using a for loop.
- **Recursion Problems** – Four recursive functions: power of three, multiple of three, power of two and perfect square.
- **Reverse List** – Reverses an array.

### CodeHS Graphics
These use CodeHS's graphics library, so they run on codehs.com rather than in Node.js.

- **Horizontal Lines** – A function that draws horizontal lines at different heights and lengths.
- **Click for Rectangles** – Draws a randomly colored rectangle wherever you click.
- **Graphics Stop Light** – Draws a centered stoplight with red, yellow and green lights.
- **Pool Table** – A function that draws numbered pool balls in any color and position.
